`Render task ids in the list`. The third one, `Add in-memory store (draft)`,

must not survive.

- it is a linear history (no merge commits) sitting directly on top of
  `seed/r3-remote`;
- it adds exactly two commits, in this order: `Add request metrics`, then
  `Render task ids in the list`;
- `api/db.js` is upstream's version, with `remove()` intact.

commit and why that is undesirable for a branch you are about to push.
